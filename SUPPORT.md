# Getting help

Different questions have different homes. Picking the right one is the
difference between an answer today and a thread nobody sees.

## Something is broken

Open an issue on the repository it happens in, using the bug template. Include
what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A version number
and the exact error text are worth more than a paragraph of description.

If you are not sure which repository, open it against the app you were using.
We move issues; you do not have to get this right.

## You want something that does not exist

Two ways, and they do different things.

Propose it on the [feature board](https://oxy.so/features). It becomes a real
issue in the right repository, other people can vote for it, and the vote count
moves its priority. Search first: the board shows you similar proposals before
you file, including ones already shipped or already declined with a reason.

Or open an issue directly with the feature template, if you would rather write
it yourself.

## You have a question

Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/OxyHQ/discussions). Questions asked
there help the next person; questions asked in a private message help exactly
one.

For how something works, start with the [help centre](https://oxy.so/help) and
the [academy](https://oxy.so/academy).

## You found a security vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue.** Use "Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab
of the affected repository, which is private and keeps the report attached to
the code, or email security@oxy.so. Full policy in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## You want to use Oxy commercially

The applications are under the Breathe License: free to use and modify provided
you publish your changes and credit us, paid if you make money from it.
Cooperatives, nonprofits and educational institutions pay nothing. The SDK
layer is Apache-2.0 and free for anyone, including commercially.

Write to licensing@oxy.so. Details in
[LICENSE-BREATHE.md](./LICENSE-BREATHE.md).

## Something about your account

Account, privacy and data questions go to support@oxy.so rather than a public
issue, since answering usually means discussing your account.

## What we cannot promise

Oxy is built by a very small number of people. Issues are read, not always
answered quickly, and an issue that goes quiet has not been rejected. If
something is urgent and you have a commercial agreement with us, say so in your
message.
